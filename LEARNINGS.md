## 1. The five complete breaks (proved, not argued)

### 1.1 TSH-512: free collisions from padding marker overwrite (seq 13)

**Break:** `TSH(X) == TSH(X || 0x80)` for **every** X with
`len(X) ≡ 30 (mod 32)`. Measured at lengths 30/62/94/126; exhaustive
over all 256 single-byte extensions, only `0x80` collides.

**Mechanism:** the 10*1 padding appends `0x80`, zero-fills to a 32-byte
multiple, then **overwrites the final byte with `0x01`** — so the
domain marker is destroyed exactly when it lands on a block's last
byte. `pad(X)` and `pad(X||0x80)` become byte-identical.

**Lesson (old, re-proved painfully):** a 24-round permutation cannot
save a broken padding. The frozen fixture ships the colliding pair as
`known_collision_tsh512` — the defect is part of the permanent record.

### 1.2 T1-v1: nine invariant subspaces (seq 34)

**Break:** one input-trit flip changed only ~7% of output trits
(avalanche gate wants [0.6, 0.73]; T1 measured 0.0742), and 54 output
positions were unreachable in 400 flips. A per-round differential
trace showed the difference **never leaving 3 of 27 lanes in 18
rounds**.

**Mechanism:** theta mixed lane triples `{c, c+9, c+18}` (same residue
mod 9), and pi was affine `l → 7l+3 (mod 27)`. **Any affine lane
permutation maps each residue class mod 9 into a single residue
class** — so no round component ever mixed classes. The state
decomposed into nine independent 18-trit worlds; the rate lanes see
one lane per class ⇒ ~1/9 × 2/3 ≈ 7% output avalanche.

**Generalizable rule (the newest thing on this timeline):** in a
width-n state with a linear layer mixing stride-s groups, an affine
permutation with any slope preserves residue classes mod (n/s).
*Check at build time, by measurement, that the group generated by your
linear layer's support and your permutation acts transitively on
positions.* This is the PRINTcipher invariant-subspace family (2011),
resurfacing in a ternary sponge — caught only because the avalanche
gate was run before any argument was believed.

**Fix (v2, seq 53):** theta over adjacent triples `{3g, 3g+1, 3g+2}`
(consecutive residues cross classes). Measured after fix: trace escapes
its founding triple by round 2, all 27 lanes by round 6; avalanche
0.6669 with 0/486 out of band.

### 1.3 T2-v1/v2: the dead pipe (seq 43)

**Break:** state-level change rate was a constant 0.3339 at **every**
round count 2→10 — 96 of 192 pipe trits never changed under message
differences. Pipe B (`h[16..31]`) was initialized, read once in
feed-forward, and **never written**: every G-step wrote indices 0–15
only.

**Why it mattered twice over:** (a) the effective chaining value was
96 trits (~152 bits), halving any collision ceiling a "192-trit state"
claim would imply; (b) **output-level avalanche was perfect (0.667,
0/486 out of band) at every round count** — the digest-level gate
could not distinguish 2-round from 10-round T2, nor see half the state
inert (see §2.1).

**Fix (v3, seq 65):** 16 bidirectional G-steps per round (columns AND
diagonals on both pipes) + per-block cross-mix. Measured: state mean
0.6673, min 0.6345, zero positions below 0.6 — and still fully
diffused at rounds=4. Cost: ~2.2× throughput (recorded, rank 4).

### 1.4 T3: the 3-adic kernel of ring arithmetic (seq 46, 74)

**Break:** for the round function
`f(x) = a₂x² + a₁x + a₀ (mod 729)` over the Z/729 ring,
`f(x + 243) == f(x)` for **exactly 243/729 = 1/3 of all x** whenever
`a₂ ≢ 0 (mod 3)` — measured exhaustively over real key-chain values;
162× above random.

**Mechanism:** `f(x+243) − f(x) = 243·(2a₂x + a₁) (mod 729)` vanishes
iff `2a₂x + a₁ ≡ 0 (mod 3)` — solvable for 1/3 of x. The 3-adic
filtration of 729 = 3⁶ is a differential highway.

**Fix attempt (v3):** evaluate the polynomial over the **field
GF(3⁶)** (tryte = degree-5 poly over GF(3), irreducible
p(x) = x⁶ + x⁵ + x⁴ + 1 selected by exhaustive sieve — the sieve count
116 exactly matches the theoretical (3⁶−3³−3²+3)/6, and the modulus is
re-verified at every module import). **Kernel died to exactly random:
1/729** at every measured position (seq 74).

**And the deeper lesson:** avalanche still failed (6/486 positions,
down from 326) and the state gate still failed (256/324 below floor) —
because T3's *mixing graph* (each position couples only to its i+2
neighbor; 16 rounds cannot equilibrate 27-position halves) is an
independent axis from its algebra. **Field arithmetic is necessary but
not sufficient; nonlinearity and diffusion fail separately.** T3 was
dropped from the merge by its pre-registered rule (seq 75).

### 1.5 All constructions: tick-domain replay (seq 37, 53)

**Break:** `H(msg, t) == H(msg, t + 729)` identically for all four
constructions — the tick (domain tag) was folded through tryte wrap,
so domain separation was only mod-729 deep.

**Fix (v2):** two-tryte tick binding in the padding. Measured: replay
closed at 729 — and **reappears exactly at 729² = 531,441** (tick_hi
wraps), as pre-registered before the fix was measured (seq 50). The
hole was moved and its new position published, not hidden:
`H(msg, t) == H(msg, t + 531441)` is printed in the bundle's claims
header.

**Rule:** domain tags must be full-width or hash-bound. A modularly
encoded tag is replay-shallow at its modulus.

## 2. Structural findings that changed how we measure

### 2.1 Output-ruler blindness behind a chained expander (seq 43/44)

T2-v2 and T4 expand a small chaining state to 486 output trits through
`c = wrap(c·7 + w[j%16] + seed)`. Measured consequences:

- T2 with a **completely inert pipe** had perfect digest-level
  avalanche (0.667, 0/486 out of band).
- T4 at 2 compression passes: output 0/486 out of band while the
  chaining state had **42/96 trits below 0.6** — the expander masks
  compression quality.
- T2-v3 at 2 rounds vs 10 rounds: digest statistics indistinguishable
  (seq 70).

**Rule:** statistical gates belong on the **chaining value**, not just
the digest. This timeline added a state-level diffusion gate
(pre-registered ruler: mean ∈ [0.6, 0.73], every state trit ≥ 0.5,
self-checking pipeline replication that fails loudly on drift). This is
Curl's lesson ("looked fine statistically") with the mechanism made
explicit and the countermeasure demonstrated.

### 2.2 The right ruler (and why ternary needs it stated)

Balanced ternary's neutral point is **2/3 per trit, not 1/2**. The
healthy constructions sit at 0.666–0.667 — measured repeatedly. The
original prototype's gate passed at ±0.05 (5× looser than the ±0.01
law); re-graded at the lawful ruler, TSH/PDH/blake2b all still passed
(50.11% / 50.19% / 49.99% on the binary ruler — seq 14). Grading a
ternary construction on the binary ruler (or vice versa) produces
garbage verdicts in both directions.

### 2.3 GF(3⁶) is buildable and self-verifying (seq 74)

The tryte-vm's arithmetic is ring arithmetic (wrap(a·b) mod 729); the
SPEC's "GF(3⁶)" needed actual field code: schoolbook trit polynomial
multiply + reduction mod x⁶+x⁵+x⁴+1. The irreducibility sieve over
all monic candidates *counts* 116 — matching theory exactly — so the
selection verifies itself. (Two of my own attempts at fancier
irreducibility tests were wrong first; the dumb sieve with a provable
completeness argument — any factorization of 6 has a part ≤ 3 — is the
one that worked.)

## 3. Methodology findings (the most reusable part)

### 3.1 Controls caught four instrument bugs — keep every one

1. **seq 8/12:** the collision instrument counted duplicate *inputs*
   as collisions (length-1 inputs draw from 256 values; ~50 draws
   birthday-repeat ~5 times). The blake2b control failed 4/5000 —
   impossible for a sound hash — which is how it was caught. Post-fix:
   control 0/4996, sensitivity control (16-bit truncated blake2b)
   detects 181/4996 ≈ theory.
2. **seq 32:** avalanche rates > 1.0 are impossible — my gate divided
   all positions by position-0's count instead of the trial count. The
   absurd output self-flagged it.
3. **seq 32:** the tamper gate read "INVISIBLE" for four tampers
   because same-length rewrites of one file path silently reused
   **stale `__pycache__` bytecode** — it was executing a *previous*
   tamper's code. Fix: unique path + module name per tamper, no
   bytecode writing.
4. **seq 47:** a padding-injectivity probe showed 326 "collisions" —
   all duplicate (message, tick) *pairs* (birthday of the pair space).
   The deduped rerun: 0 real collisions in 39,017 distinct pairs.

**Rule:** every collision-counting instrument dedupes inputs, carries
a control, and gets its absurd outputs investigated rather than
reported.

### 3.2 Cross-language vectors caught three real port bugs

1. TSH harness: empty input arrives as `"tsh512 "` — `line.trim()`
   destroyed the separator (harness bug, fixed).
2. **u64-as-i64 reinterpretation (seq 67):** porting IV16 constants
   derived from unbounded Python integers, `0xD131…u64 as i64`
   reinterprets mod 2⁶⁴ — and **2⁶⁴ ≢ 0 (mod 729)**, so every affected
   tryte constant silently shifted. T2/T4 mismatched on every vector
   while T1/T3 matched — the failure pattern isolated the bug instantly.
   Fix: wrap in u64 arithmetic preserving the mathematical value.
3. **GF(3⁶) modulus label mismatch (seq 74):** Python's sieve-verified
   poly was x⁶+x⁵+x⁴+1; the Rust constant was written from a
   mislabeled comment as x⁶+x⁵+x+1. Vectors caught it; canon says a
   port that disagrees on one trit is wrong, not "different".

### 3.3 Pre-registration ledger (41 predictions, all kept)

24 confirmed, 15 falsified, 2 open. The falsifications were at least
as informative as the confirmations — e.g., predicting T1-v2's
avalanche would pass only marginally (falsified: comfortable 0.65
minimum), predicting T2-v3's state would lag (falsified: zero
positions below 0.6), predicting exact differential characteristics at
reduced rounds (falsified: null differentials everywhere — the first
genuine *resistance* evidence). **Predictions recorded after results
are worthless; predictions that survive being wrong are the map.**

### 3.4 Fitness is lexicographic and it decided the merge

Truth > gates > resistance > economy. T4 became the family standard
*only* at rank 4 (0.123 MB/s vs 0.0032 / 0.0008), after all three
candidates tied at ranks 1–2 with stated rank-3 budgets — with the
honest nuance recorded that T1-v2 has the best-*characterized*
resistance margin (differential transition measured at round 4/18)
and is the designated alternate (seq 87).

## 5. What is honestly NOT here

- No new attack technique — everything used (differential tracing,
  invariant-subspace detection by measurement, kernel counting,
  truncated-prefix birthday) is standard methodology, competently
  applied.
- No deployable construction — T4-v2 is a research standard with
  published bounds (tick replay at 531,441; the expander flagged as
  the expected first outside target, seq 84), not a blake2b
  replacement.
- No PQ advance — ternary is orthogonal to post-quantum, as IGNITION
  states.
- Open docket: native (Rust) economy re-bench (the Python-scale
  ranking is instrument-labeled, seq 85); outside attack results;
  a T3 successor would need field arithmetic **plus** dense position
  mixing.

The value claim of this timeline rests where IGNITION put it: value
forms only where attackers fail. The surface is `publish/`. Attack it.

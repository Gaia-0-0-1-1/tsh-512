## Figure 1 — The sparse cross grid (E44, seq 229–230)

Direct grok counts (of 4 seeds) for cyclic composites T_k(T_j).
Only adjacent-modulus pairs grok; the diagonal (self-composites,
from E43) is excluded here and shown in Figure 2.

| outer \ inner | T5 | T6 | T7 | T8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T5** | — | **4/4** | 0/4 | 0/4 |
| **T6** | 1/4 | — | **2/4** | 0/4 |
| **T7** | 1/4 | 0/4 | — | 0/4 |
| **T8** | 1/4 | 0/4 | 0/4 | — |

The two grokking cells are adjacent moduli (|k−j| = 1); all eight
non-adjacent cells wall (≤1/4). Structural mismatch is real even
between structurally identical (cyclic) families.

## Figure 2 — The entropy/alignment onset (E41–E43, seq 223–227)

Self-composites, grok counts (seeds as noted), against composite
label entropy in bits:

| family | label H (bits) | grok | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Z4(Z4) | 2.00 | 2/2 | E42 n-sweep |
| Z5(Z5) | 2.32 | 2/2 | E42 n-sweep |
| Z6(Z6) | 2.58 | 1/2 | E42; coin-flip (see Fig. 4) |
| T5(T5) | 2.32 | 4/4 | E43 |
| T6(T6) | 2.58 | 4/4 | E43 — same entropy as Z6, 4x the groks |
| T7(T7) | 2.81 | 4/4 | E43 — the phoneme itself fails; composite groks |
| T8(T8) | 3.00 | 3/4 | E43 + pooled E32 = 4/6 |
| PD(PD) | 3.00 | **0/4** | E43 — same entropy as T8, scrambled labels |

The decisive pair is T8 vs PD at identical 3.0 bits: alignment, not
entropy, closes the wall. Z6 vs T6 at identical 2.58 bits shows the
same effect one level down (1/2 vs 4/4): Z6's composite is a group
table on its whole domain (no collapse), while T6's composite has
two dead classes (6, 7 never appear) — collapse somewhere in the
stack buys learnability back, exactly the E40/E41 law.
